About Me

Nogol Zakerian is an independent Iranian musician, violinist, vocalist, singer-songwriter, composer, and arranger working in the realm of alternative art pop.

With a background in classical violin, she brings together the depth and intricacy of classical music with the textures and expressive possibilities of contemporary pop and electronic music. Her work is characterized by richly layered arrangements, unexpected sonic combinations, emotional honesty, and an intimate vocal presence.

Drawing on her musical knowledge, diverse influences, and personal experiences, Nogol writes, composes, arranges, and produces her own music, continuously developing a distinctive musical language of her own.

“For me, making music is a way of giving form to the intensity and complexity of being human.”

Tas.Hang

Five Audiovisual Works

Tas.Hang is a personal series of five short audiovisual works exploring the relationship between sound and image.

The world of images has always felt more intuitive to me. As a child, I used to draw a lot. Creating a musical world, on the other hand, has always felt more difficult and challenging. Ideas can be hard to come by; developing them, giving them coherence and form, is difficult, and sharing them with the world can be the hardest part.

And yet, images were no longer enough. I wanted them to have sound in order to come alive and engage with more dimensions of experience and existence. Tas.Hang grew from this point — from an attempt to bring these two worlds together.

Each work began with an image, a feeling, a question, or an inner landscape, gradually developing into a space where sound and image interact — sometimes complementing one another, sometimes offering different perspectives, and sometimes deliberately leaving room for ambiguity.

The series became part of my process of experimentation and growth in working with image, sound, and narrative: five small worlds, each open to being seen, heard, and experienced from a different perspective.

The Five Works

01
We Could Not Last Down There

A reflection on the connection between the material world and an unseen world of consciousness.

02
Mother Encounter

A poetic encounter with darkness, earth, warmth, and light.

03
My Little World

A work about leaving, confinement, and the need to keep moving when stillness becomes a kind of death.

04
I Saw Something in the Water

A journey inward, toward a place where past and future meet in the present.

05
Come Out, Girl

A movement from confinement toward freedom, shaped by the weight of responsibilities that had become too large.
